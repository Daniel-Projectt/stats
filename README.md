# Statistics for Business — exam on chapters 4, 5 and 6

Study page in the parchment style of the other study guides.

**Live:** https://daniel-projectt.github.io/stats/

- **Guide**: the 31 objectives from the three "Chapter Objectives" sheets, in their words, with a one-breath answer each.
- **Exam Kit**: a flashcard worth bringing (the exam allows one), step-by-step use of the two allowed tools (Normal Distribution Explorer, Sampling Distribution Explorer), and a which-method table.
- **Chapters 4, 5, 6**: notes per objective, flashcards, match, quizzes (169 questions).
- **Practice Exam**: by chapter and type, plus "The 62" (two from every objective).

Build: `sh build.sh` (assembles `index.html` from `src/`, runs `src/test.js`, which recomputes every number on the page); browser test: `node src/test-dom.js <dir with jsdom>`.
